# Maritime Surveillance

In the naval domain, ARTEMIS.IA algorithms are used for maritime surveillance to combat hybrid threats and trafficking.

**Key Capabilities:**
- Cross-references AIS (Automatic Identification System) ship positioning signals, meteorological data, and optical or radar satellite imagery.
- Automatically detects illicit navigation behaviors, such as the deliberate deactivation of beacons or clandestine transshipments on the high seas.
