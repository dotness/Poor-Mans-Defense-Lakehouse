# Arcadia (Command & Control Platform)

The Arcadia Project is a sovereign, artificial intelligence-assisted command platform designed to provide a next-generation Command and Control (C2) tool for the French military.

**Key Capabilities:**
- Employs a decentralized "mesh network" model instead of a centralized architecture, allowing command posts to be redundantly linked to field servers.
- Drastically improves system resilience against electronic jamming, destruction of communication nodes, or massive cyberattacks.
- Ensures digital interoperability with NATO allies.
- Will form part of a comprehensive and sovereign "combat cloud".
- Integrates with "digital assistants" for staff officers (like Comand AI), cutting the time-consuming preparation of operations orders by three or four times, thereby providing critical decision-making support in the command loop.
