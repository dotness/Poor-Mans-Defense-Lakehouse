# Escrim (Intelligence Centralization)

Escrim is the DRM's (Directorate of Military Intelligence) specific business application natively hosted on the ARTEMIS.IA infostructure.

**Key Capabilities:**
- Centralizes all Joint Intelligence Function (FIR) databases.
- Integrates machine learning algorithms to relieve analysts of repetitive tasks.
- Automates anomaly detection.
- Provides text translation capabilities.
- Performs pattern recognition on satellite imagery.
- Automates massive ingestion and cross-referencing of OSINT data from social networks along with SIGINT, IMINT, and HUMINT.
- Assists in geopolitical analysis and visualizing networks.
