# Operational Planning and Predictive Maintenance (Operational Condition Maintenance - MCO)

ARTEMIS.IA applies artificial intelligence to operational planning and the Operational Condition Maintenance of military equipment (SCORPION vehicles, aircraft, ships).

**Key Capabilities:**
- Aids in operational planning (la planification).
- Continuously analyzes telemetry and mechanical data from integrated sensors (Internet of Military Things - IoMT).
- Anticipates mechanical failures before they occur.
- Uses machine learning algorithms to diagnose the causes of anomalies.
- Optimizes revision scheduling.
- Accurately adjusts spare part stocks to avoid costly surpluses and critical supply shortages.
- Significantly reduces unplanned downtime, cuts maintenance costs, and increases equipment operational availability and safety.
