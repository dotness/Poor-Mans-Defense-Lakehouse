# Sovereign LLMs (e.g., Berthier-Mistral-Military-24B)

The ARTEMIS.IA system encompasses the integration of sovereign Large Language Models (LLMs) specialized for Defense to exploit massive amounts of textual data (doctrines, notes, manuals, reports).

**Key Capabilities:**
- Pre-trained on an immense multilingual corpus of military doctrine (including NATO standardization agreements, military medicine publications, and French All-Arms Texts).
- Overcomes the "hallucinations" of commercial generative AIs by accurately identifying highly specific Defense acronyms and ontology.
- Automates the drafting of joint summary notes.
- Translates and routes multilingual operational documents within coalitions.
- Implements Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architectures to allow users to intuitively query doctrinal knowledge bases.
