The Asymmetry of Innovation Cycles and Obsolescence The main risk, highlighted by parliamentary reports (notably the critical opinion formulated by MP Fabien Gouttefarde in 2021), lies in the fundamental time lag between the heavy development cycles of state armaments programs and the incredible speed of civilian digital technological evolution. Building a "centralized data processing factory" takes many years. Between the initial specifications drafted in 2017 and the operational deployment of Escrim in 2023, global software architectures were revolutionized (emergence of Transformer architectures, breakthroughs in generative AI). The highlighted risk was delivering a monolithic tool to the armed forces that was already algorithmically obsolete right when it entered service. Although the ministry attempted to thwart this structural pitfall by requiring the ATHEA joint venture to provide a modular and open architecture (via the SDK development kit promoting the integration of third-party software), the challenge of permanently integrating innovative building blocks into pre-existing military Information Systems (IS) ("legacy systems") remains extremely complex in both theory and practice.

The Battle for Human Resources and Attractiveness Technological superiority is only effective if the Ministry of Armed Forces has the engineers to develop it and the analysts to exploit it. The "war for talent" is raging. The ministry is in frontal competition with the private sector (the American GAFAM tech giants and European unicorns) for the recruitment of extremely rare profiles: data scientists, cloud architects, cybersecurity experts, and machine learning specialists. Acculturating staffs to the logic of coding, and training DRM or DRSD analysts, represent a very long-term institutional endeavor. The ministry, via the creation of AMIAD, is attempting to mitigate this attractiveness deficit by making its contracts more flexible, modernizing its work environments, and banking on intellectual engagement, patriotic fiber, and exclusive access to secret data that is impossible to process in the civilian world.
